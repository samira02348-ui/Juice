Our story

Chicken Nation started with one simple idea: Bamenda deserves fried chicken that's hot, fresh and fairly priced. Every piece is seasoned in-house, fried to order, and served with a smile — whether you're dining in, grabbing takeaway, or getting it delivered to your door.

  • Fresh off the fryer — never sitting under a heat lamp.
  • Fast — your food, hot, in minutes.
  • Friendly & fair — great service at prices everyone can afford.
